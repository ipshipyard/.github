> [!IMPORTANT]
> Shipyard [ended its IPFS work](https://ipshipyard.com/blog/2026-the-end-of-ipfs-at-shipyard/) on September 30, 2026, and the team has dissolved.
> Do not expect replies to issues, pull requests, or mentions in our repositories.
>
> For support, and to organize community self-hosting of any infrastructure you need, go to the [IPFS Forums](https://discuss.ipfs.tech/).

Between 2024 and 2026, [we were an independent engineering team](https://ipshipyard.com/) maintaining, operating, and developing
many of the important tools and services in the [IPFS](https://ipfs.tech/) and [Libp2p](https://libp2p.io/) ecosystem.
